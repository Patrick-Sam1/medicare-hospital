Surgical Services

Comprehensive surgical expertise across multiple specialties, delivering quality care and comfort from pre-op through recovery.

Our highly trained surgical services team are ready to provide surgical expertise, quality care as well as comfort to all patients throughout the entire surgical experience. MediCare Plus Hospital surgical services include:

  • General Surgery
  • Neuro Surgery
  • Orthopaedic Surgery
  • ENT (Ear, Nose & Throat Surgery)
  • Urology
  • Anaesthesiology
  • Ophthalmology
  • Endoscopy
  • Breast Surgery
  • Cardiothoracic Surgery
  • Cardiovascular Surgery
  • Gastroenterology & Gastrointestinal Surgery
  • Gynaecological Surgery
  • Colorectal and Laparoscopic Surgery
