Career

Join our team of dedicated healthcare professionals and be part of a world-class medical institution committed to excellence in patient care.

Home / News Hub / Career

Welcome and thank you for your interest in a career with MediCare Plus Hospital Group. We are always looking for exceptional team members. Find fulfilling career opportunities at MediCare Plus Hospital Group, and join us in providing a comprehensive first-world medical service exceeding all expectations of our valued patients. We encourage applications from all qualified individuals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What positions are currently available?

We have openings across various departments including:

  • Nursing (All Specialties)
  • Medical Doctors (Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, OB/GYN)
  • Allied Health Professionals
  • Administration and Management
  • Support Staff
  • Laboratory and Diagnostic Services

Please check our current openings page for specific vacancies.

How do I apply for a position?

You can submit your application by:

  • Sending your CV and cover letter to info@medicareplus.com
  • Visiting our Human Resources department at our Victoria Island office

Please ensure you include your current CV, a cover letter, and any relevant certifications.

What are the benefits of working at MediCare Plus?

We offer a comprehensive benefits package including:

  • Competitive salary and performance bonuses
  • Health insurance coverage
  • Professional development and training opportunities
  • Career advancement pathways
  • Paid time off and holidays
  • Pension and retirement plans
Do you offer internships or training programs?

Yes, we offer internship programs for:

  • Medical students
  • Nursing students
  • Allied health students
  • Administrative interns

We also provide continuous professional development for our staff through workshops, conferences, and in-house training programs.
