Our Resident Physicians

Meet the experienced medical professionals who make MediCare Plus Hospital a centre of excellence

Dr. Olumide Adebayo
Consultant Cardiologist
MBBS, FMCS, FICS, PhD (Spain) Dr. Adebayo completed his medical training at the University College Hospital, Ibadan in 1988 and postgraduate training in General and Breast Surgery from the University of Navarre Teaching Hospital, Pamplona, Spain in 1997. He received his Doctorate degree in Surgery in 1998 from the same institution. He is a fellow at the Medical College of Surgeons of Nigeria and The International College of Surgeons. He has vast experience in the management of benign and malignant conditions of the breast. Dr. Adebayo has interest in Surgical Oncology (Breast, GIT Tumors, and Thyroid tumours), minimally invasive surgery and Quality assurance in health care.
Dr. Akinwumi Adeoye
Consultant Neurologist
FRCS, FWACS, MBBS (London) Dr. Adeoye completed his medical training at the University of London, UK in 1985 and postgraduate training in Neurology from the National Hospital for Neurology and Neurosurgery, Queen Square, London in 1992. He received his fellowship from the Royal College of Surgeons of England and the West African College of Surgeons. He has over 30 years of experience in the management of neurological disorders including stroke, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis. Dr. Adeoye has special interest in neuro-rehabilitation, neuro-critical care, and the management of neurodegenerative diseases.
Dr. Mehmet Yılmaz
Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon
MD, FACS, FRCS (Orth), PhD (Turkey) Dr. Yılmaz completed his medical degree from Hacettepe University, Ankara, Turkey in 1990 and postgraduate training in Orthopaedic Surgery from Istanbul University, Turkey in 1997. He received his Doctorate degree in Orthopaedic Surgery in 2000. He is a fellow of the American College of Surgeons and the Royal College of Surgeons. He has over 25 years of experience in orthopaedic trauma, joint replacement, and sports medicine. Dr. Yılmaz has special interest in minimally invasive joint replacement, arthroscopic surgery, and paediatric orthopaedics.
