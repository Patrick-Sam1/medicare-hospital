Our team of highly experienced consulting specialists brings world-class expertise across multiple medical disciplines to deliver exceptional patient care.

Our Consulting Specialists

World-class specialists providing expert consultation across a wide range of medical disciplines

Prof. Kamar Tayo Adeleke
President/CEO, Tristate Healthcare System
M.D., F.A.C.C., S.F.N.L.I. Professor Adeleke is a US-trained medical practitioner with over 34 years' experience as a consultant physician and interventional cardiologist. He founded the First State Diagnostic Center, Peninsula Medical Associates and Wilmington Cardiovascular Associates, all reputable industry brand leaders. He is a fellow of the American College of Cardiology and has published extensively in the field of cardiovascular medicine. Professor Adeleke has also served as a visiting professor at several prestigious medical institutions across the United States and Europe. He is widely recognized for his contributions to advancing cardiac care and medical education in Africa.
Dr. Seyi Oyesola
Critical Care Consultant
B.Sc. (Hons) MBBS, FRCAI, AFICM – A3C, Ng Dr. Oyesola obtained his basic medical qualification at the College of Medicine, University of Lagos in 1986, before going to the United Kingdom and the United States to complete post-graduate training in anesthesia. He is a fellow of the Royal College of Anaesthetists and has extensive experience in critical care medicine. Dr. Oyesola has worked in several leading intensive care units across the UK and Nigeria. He specializes in the management of complex critically ill patients and has a strong interest in patient safety and quality improvement in healthcare delivery.
Dr. Julius Olowo Ijitola
Consultant ENT Surgeon
FWACS, FMCO (ORL), Associate Fellow Dr. Ijitola is a Fellow of the West African College of Surgeons, an Associate Fellow of the Medical College of Otorhinolaryngology, and a Consultant Ear, Nose, Throat, Head and Neck Surgeon with both local and international training. He had his tertiary education at the University of Ibadan, his residency training at the University College Hospital, Ibadan and his international training at the Groote Schuur Hospital, Cape Town, South Africa. He has over 20 years of experience in managing complex ENT conditions. Dr. Ijitola has special interest in head and neck oncology and advanced endoscopic sinus surgery.
Dr. Seyi Roberts
Consultant Neurologist
MB,BS, FMCP, FWACP, CERT NEUROLOGY (HARVARD) Dr. Roberts graduated as a Medical Doctor from the University of Ibadan in June 1975. He obtained certification from the US Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates and completed his neurology training at Harvard University. He has over 40 years of experience in the management of neurological disorders. Dr. Roberts has special interest in stroke rehabilitation, epilepsy management, and neurodegenerative diseases. He has published numerous research articles in leading neurology journals and has been a keynote speaker at several international neurology conferences across the globe.
Dr. David Igbokwe
Consultant Clinical Psychologist
B.Sc. (1st Class Hons), M.Sc., Ph.D. (Clinical Psychology, Distinction) Dr. Igbokwe is a Licensed Clinical Psychologist and practicing psychotherapist. He was introduced to psychotherapy in 2001 and has continued to hone his psychotherapy skills with international and national trainings to an expert proficiency level. He is a member of several professional psychological associations including the Nigerian Psychological Association and the American Psychological Association. Dr. Igbokwe has a special interest in cognitive behavioral therapy, trauma counseling, and workplace mental health. He has also conducted extensive research on mental health in African populations.
Dr. Ronke Akinola
Consultant Paediatrician
FWACP, FESPE (Paediatric Endocrinology) Dr. Akinola had her paediatric residency training at the Lagos State University Teaching Hospital, Ikeja. She is a fellow of the West African College of Physicians and a fellow of the European Society for Paediatric Endocrinology. She has special interest in paediatric endocrinology and child development. Dr. Akinola has over 15 years of experience in the management of childhood endocrine disorders including diabetes, growth disorders, and thyroid abnormalities. She is passionate about improving child health outcomes in Nigeria and has been involved in several public health initiatives focused on child nutrition and preventive care.
